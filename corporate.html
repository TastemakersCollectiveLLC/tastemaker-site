How it works

Inquiry

You tell us the date, the place, the guest count and any dietary needs.

Consultation

We talk through the food, the service style and how the day runs.

Custom menu

We write a menu for your event and quote it.

Event day

We handle the food and the service from start to finish.

Service styles

Plated

Courses plated in the kitchen and brought to each seat.

Family style

Large shared dishes set down on the table.

Buffet

Guests serve themselves from a set line.

Stations

Separate stations, cooked and served in front of guests.

Drop-off

Delivered ready to serve, with nothing to set up.
