On set

Production and on set catering in Los Angeles

Crew meals delivered to location on your schedule.

What we do

  • Walking breakfast at call
  • Hot lunch or boxed lunch within the meal window
  • Artist and talent hospitality

How it runs

  • You send call time, meal time, crew count, location and dietary counts.
  • We deliver to location and set, or stay and serve.
  • Every dish is labeled for dietary needs and allergens.
  • Meals are timed to your meal window.

Dietary needs

Vegan, gluten free and dairy free options are built into every crew menu. Allergen counts from your sheet are matched dish by dish.

Insurance and permits

General liability insurance with a certificate naming the production as additional insured, available on request. Permitted by LA County Public Health. Prepared in a permitted commercial kitchen.
