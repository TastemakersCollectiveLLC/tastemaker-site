Frequently asked questions

How does booking work?

Send an inquiry with the date, the place, the guest count and any dietary needs. We reply with a custom menu and a quote. The menu and consultation are free.

How does the deposit work?

A 50% deposit holds the date. Events booked on short notice are paid in full at signing.

Do you offer tastings?

A tasting is available once a wedding is booked.

Can you handle dietary restrictions and allergies?

That is our specialty. Vegan, gluten free and dairy free options are on every menu, every dish is labeled for dietary needs and allergens, and halal is available on request.

How does drop-off work?

Boxed meals or pans, delivered ready to serve. Send a request on the Order page at least 48 hours ahead.

Do you deliver plated food?

No. Nothing plated or assembled is delivered. Boards and spreads are built on site by our staff.

Do you offer bar service?

Yes, as an add-on. We provide bartenders and house-made mixers; you supply the alcohol from a shopping list we give you.

Where do you work?

Los Angeles and Southern California, plus festivals across California.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We carry $2M general liability insurance and the certificate can name your event or production as additional insured.

  • Permitted by LA County Public Health.
  • Prepared in a permitted commercial kitchen.
  • $2M general liability insurance.
  • ServSafe certified manager.
  • Certificate of insurance available on request.
